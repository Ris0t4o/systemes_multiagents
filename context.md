Context:
Hiding is a skill we all learn at an early age through games like hide-and-seek.
Beyond play, it relates to real-life challenges such as camouflage, evasion, and search strategies. 
In this project, one drone acts as a seeker while others constantly hide and move behind obstacles.
Beyond its scientific relevance, this Case Study will serve as an example of mediation and vulgarisation of science, in a simple and engaging way for visitors of the Volière (hundreds of children every year).

Objective: Having a Drone as a seeker who's constantly moving and communicating its position and its orientation to every hider.

The hider's goal is to hide from the seeker between obstacles of which the position is constantly known.

Theoretical approach:
3D motion of quadcopter drones
Leader–follower consensus approach (Seeker to Hiders)
Bounded/asymptotic consensus approach (Obstacles to Hiders)
Path optimization
Path planning
Field of view
